🔒 1. Core Privacy Principle: Local Data Sovereignty

⚡ Local-first architecture
✅ No External Servers: We do not use outside servers to store, process, or backup your personal call data.
✅ On-Device Analysis: All missed call grouping, history analysis, and frequency statistics are calculated directly on your device using your local call log.
✅ Privacy by Design: Your sensitive communication history never leaves your physical control — a core selling point of our service.

📲 2. Permissions Requested and Why

To provide core call management and hands-free features, the App requires specific permissions. Here is exactly how we use them:

📞 Call and Contact Management

READ_CALL_LOG READ_CONTACTS READ_PHONE_STATE CALL_PHONE
  • READ_CALL_LOG – Essential to read missed calls and group them by caller (e.g., "James Mwangi called 3 times").
  • READ_CONTACTS – Resolves phone numbers into names from your device’s contact book.
  • READ_PHONE_STATE – Detects incoming calls in real-time to trigger management features.
  • CALL_PHONE – Enables returning missed calls directly from the app interface.

🎙️ Hands-Free & Premium Features

RECORD_AUDIO FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE FOREGROUND_SERVICE RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
  • RECORD_AUDIO & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE – Used strictly for "Go Premium" hands-free mode to assist drivers via voice commands. No audio is ever recorded to a file or sent to a server.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE – Ensures hands-free assistance or auto-redial tasks continue reliably.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Resumes scheduled auto-redial lists or updates local spam database after phone restart.

💾 Storage and Connectivity

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – Used on older Android versions (SDK ≤28) to locally store app-specific settings or bundled spam database.
  • INTERNET – Used for three specific tasks: (1) displaying a single Google AdMob banner for free tier users; (2) processing premium upgrades via Google Play Billing Library; (3) periodically downloading updated list of known spam numbers to keep local database current.

🛡️ 3. Spam Detection & Third-Party Interactions

  • Local Spam Check: Basic spam detection uses a local database bundled within the App and updated periodically.
  • Enhanced Spam Intelligence: For premium users, the App may check numbers against a cloud-based spam score with explicit disclosure. We never send your personal contacts to third-party APIs without consent.
  • Truecaller Integration: If you choose to use Truecaller lookup, the App only sends the phone number to the Truecaller app via a standard Android intent. This only happens when you manually tap the "Likely Spam" badge.

🔐 4. Data Safety and Disclosure

✅ No Data Collection: We do not collect, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties.
✅ Local Retention: All analysis is done in memory or stored in the App’s local private storage.
✅ User Control: Because we use sensitive permissions like READ_CALL_LOG, we provide clear onboarding to explain that your call data never leaves the device unless you opt-in to specific premium spam features.

🇪🇺 Information about GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

We may be collecting and using information from you if you are from the European Economic Area (EEA). This section explains how and why data is collected, and how we maintain protection.

What is GDPR? EU-wide privacy law that regulates how EU residents' data is protected and enhances individual control. At Call Manager, we implement GDPR controls as baseline standard for all operations worldwide.

Personal data under GDPR includes any information relating to an identified or identifiable person (name, IP, biometric data, etc.). The data protection principles require fairness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, and storage limits. Data subjects have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, and data portability.
